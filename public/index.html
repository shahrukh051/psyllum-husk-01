FAQ

Questions we hear daily.

Straight answers about our product, ingredients, and how to use them.

How do I take psyllium husk?

Mix one serving (approx. 5g) into a full glass of water, juice, or smoothie and drink immediately. Follow with another glass of water. Take once daily, preferably before a meal.

Is it safe to take every day?

Yes. Psyllium husk is a well-studied, soluble dietary fibre recognised as safe for daily use. Each batch is lab-tested for purity. As with any supplement, consult a healthcare professional if you have existing conditions.

What makes Husk & Co. different?

Our non-dust vegetable fibre formula means finer, cleaner milling — no gritty texture, no coughing. Every batch is certified organic, lab-tested, and blended in small runs so you always get the freshest product.

Can I mix it with food or smoothies?

Absolutely. The Unflavored blend is especially popular for baking and smoothies. The flavoured blends taste great in water or milk, but feel free to experiment — it works well stirred into oatmeal and yoghurt too.

Do you ship across India?

Yes, we ship pan-India. Delivery typically takes 3–7 business days. First-order shipping is free. Bulk orders of 3+ pouches also qualify for free shipping.
